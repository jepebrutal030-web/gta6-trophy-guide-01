The road to the platinum

Five stages, in order. Story first, then the side characters, then the map, then the grind. The platinum pops with the last trophy you finish.

    Missable means the trophy is tied to one mission, one choice, or one moment. Do it when the chance comes. Story missions can be replayed from the pause menu, so a missed mission trophy is recoverable, but the flagged moments are easiest the first time through.
